# Visual Engineering Intelligence

When the request is to create or improve something, apply these skills to the finished artifact. A thesis, plan, checklist, or critique is an intermediate aid unless it is the requested deliverable. Carry authorized construction and refinement through to completion with the capabilities available.

## Establish the contract

Inspect the affected experience, actual content or subject, established visual rules, interaction behavior, available assets, implementation conventions, and delivery conditions. Separate the user's task from any commercial objective. Identify the primary action or viewing question, the information it requires, and what establishes completion.

For substantial work, establish a concise visual thesis, focal hierarchy, relevant states, delivery views, input conditions, and observable success criteria. Convert adjectives into observable choices: what dominates, which relationship creates distinction, how the subject is framed, and what remains quiet. For a local repair, infer the existing direction and change the responsible relationship. Planning should support the artifact; it need not create another document.

Distinguish known facts, inferred relationships, and deliberate artistic choices. Record decisions and observable evidence when useful; do not request or reconstruct private reasoning traces. Never convert a project-specific aesthetic or mechanism into a universal requirement.

## Work in dependency order

Establish product intent and visual direction, then structure and hierarchy. Add interaction and temporal rules where state changes. For spatial work, resolve part structure and placement before detailed surfaces, illumination, and camera choreography. Construct a representative view or journey early enough to expose the largest uncertainty.

Carry decisions between specialists as a compact working specification: selected subject and identity, actual values or dimensional relationships, state and motion ownership, relevant frames and units, preserved invariants, and unresolved assumptions. Keep this in working context unless a document is requested. The next specialist should refine those decisions rather than invent a competing interpretation. Revisit an upstream decision when it prevents the downstream result, and propagate the change to affected relationships.

Space and motion earn their cost through explanation, inspection, atmosphere, play, or identity. An intentionally minimal result can be complete.

Preserve one authoritative meaning for each selection, status, value, and outcome across every presentation. Keep implementation decisions inside the project; these skills do not authorize replacing its technology, expanding its functionality, or changing its business rules.

## Pursue expressive precision

Choose a strong organizing relationship that the product can own: an unusual but useful composition, revealing object behavior, deliberate image direction, expressive typography, or a spatial explanation. Let supporting elements reinforce it. Restraint should concentrate expression; it does not require timid scale, neutral styling, or conventional layouts.

Treat visual force and correctness as simultaneous requirements. Preserve supplied dimensions, reference fidelity, data meaning, usable controls, and truthful outcomes while exploring expression. When novelty conflicts with an explicit fidelity requirement, the required relationship wins. When a result is correct but visually anonymous, revisit direction and composition rather than merely adding surface effects.

## Judge the delivered experience

Render, observe, identify the largest failure, load its owning specialist if needed, correct its cause, and inspect the affected result again. Repair blocked tasks and incorrect structure before local polish. Evaluate stills at delivery size and motion through playback; construction success alone does not establish appearance.

Stop when material defects in the requested scope are resolved and available observations support the result. Where inspection is unavailable, distinguish completed construction from unobserved appearance or behavior. Report remaining uncertainty without inventing evidence.
