# Site archetypes

Starting points for common premium briefs. An archetype is a map of what usually matters, not a template: the real subject, content and brand decide. Pick the closest archetype, then run the world, directions and anti-generic steps in [award craft](../../award-craft/SKILL.md). Face names refer to the [font library](../../typography-system/references/font-library.md); palettes to the [palette library](../../color-system/references/palette-library.md).

Each entry lists: audience and job · world ideas · structure · type · palette · imagery · signature ideas · motion character · must-have components · pitfalls.

## 1. Fine watches and jewelry

- **Job:** collectors and gift buyers judge craft, compare references, book an appointment or buy.
- **World:** the movement, the bench, the loupe, hallmarks, facets.
- **Structure:** object-led hero → macro detail sequence → specification sheet → craft story → collection index → boutique or appointment.
- **Type:** optical-size serif (Newsreader, Bodoni Moda; GT Sectra or Canela when licensed) with a restrained grotesque (Schibsted Grotesk, Suisse Int'l); tabular figures for specifications.
- **Palette:** Graphite Atelier or Midnight Chrome; one precious accent taken from the material.
- **Imagery:** macro photography, controlled reflections, one light direction.
- **Signature:** scroll-driven exploded movement (SVG or procedural 3D); a loupe cursor over macro shots with a keyboard alternative; a dial that retints the page by time of day.
- **Motion:** heavy and precise, no overshoot, stepped rotations, long holds on specifications.
- **Components:** reference table, strap and size configurator, appointment booking, boutique locator.
- **Pitfalls:** gold on black by reflex, fake scarcity, tiny gray specification text.

## 2. Fashion house and luxury apparel

- **Job:** discover the collection, feel the attitude, find a look, buy or visit.
- **World:** the atelier, pattern paper, the runway, fabric swatches, the lookbook.
- **Structure:** campaign image or film → collection index → lookbook → product detail → stores.
- **Type:** statement display (PP Editorial New, Bodoni Moda at display size, ABC Whyte or Druk when licensed; Italiana or Big Shoulders Display free) with a neutral text face.
- **Palette:** Ink & Paper Mono or a single seasonal color block; product color accuracy first.
- **Imagery:** full-bleed campaign photography, unexpected crops, editorial pairings of detail and silhouette.
- **Signature:** cursor-driven fabric distortion on the lookbook (WebGL), a runway horizontal passage, looks that expand into product pages with shared-element transitions.
- **Motion:** confident, fast cuts, clip reveals, no bounce.
- **Components:** look grid with hover alternate image, size selector with guide, wishlist, store locator.
- **Pitfalls:** unreadable type over busy photos, heavy video hero on mobile, hidden prices.

## 3. Fragrance, beauty and skincare

- **Job:** imagine a sensation, understand notes or ingredients, choose, buy or order samples.
- **World:** maceration and notes, ingredients, the flacon, the ritual, the lab (for clinical skincare).
- **Structure:** product and sensation hero → notes or ingredient story → rituals → range → discovery set.
- **Type:** soft optical serif (Fraunces with SOFT axis, Newsreader) or clean clinical sans (Instrument Sans, Hanken Grotesk) depending on sensory versus clinical positioning.
- **Palette:** colors sampled from ingredients (orris, resin, citrus) or clinical whites with one skin-tone-safe accent; Pastel Lab for playful beauty.
- **Imagery:** macro ingredients, liquids, glass; consistent warm or cool grade.
- **Signature:** procedural color field that follows the notes; liquid transitions; texture swatches that respond to the pointer.
- **Motion:** slow, diffused, heavy springs on the bottle, dissolves rather than slides.
- **Components:** notes pyramid, ingredient glossary, shade finder, sample set builder.
- **Pitfalls:** didone, cream and gold by reflex; claims without sources; low-contrast pastel text.

## 4. Architecture and interior studio

- **Job:** clients and press judge the work, understand the approach, contact the studio.
- **World:** the section drawing, the plan, materials, light through a day.
- **Structure:** one project as the opening → project index → project pages (drawings, photos, facts) → practice → contact.
- **Type:** precise grotesque (Suisse Int'l, ABC Diatype; Schibsted Grotesk or Hanken Grotesk free) with a quiet serif for writing (Sentient, Source Serif 4).
- **Palette:** Travertine or Ink & Paper Mono; color comes from the photographs.
- **Imagery:** large architectural photography with consistent verticals; drawings as line work.
- **Signature:** line drawing traces then the photograph fills it; a section-cut clip transition; a project index that previews on hover and focus.
- **Motion:** architectural and calm, long crossfades, measured reveals, no playful physics.
- **Components:** filterable project index, project fact sheet, image sequence with captions, team, press.
- **Pitfalls:** tiny captions, gallery-only pages without facts, heavy uncompressed imagery.

## 5. Hospitality: hotels, resorts, restaurants

- **Job:** feel the place, check availability, book a room or a table.
- **World:** the corridor of doors, the key, the table setting, day into night, the landscape.
- **Structure:** place hero with booking entry → rooms → dining → experiences → location → booking.
- **Type:** warm serif (Gloock, Young Serif, Zodiak) with a friendly sans (Figtree, General Sans).
- **Palette:** Forest Lodge, Sand Dune or colors sampled from the landscape.
- **Imagery:** real rooms and people in natural light; video with posters.
- **Signature:** scroll-driven day-to-night lighting; rooms as doors along a horizontal passage; a map that draws the journey.
- **Motion:** unhurried, soft easing, parallax limited to one depth layer.
- **Components:** date-range booking widget, room comparison, menus as accessible text, map, offers.
- **Pitfalls:** booking hidden below the fold, PDF menus, autoplay sound, generic drone footage.

## 6. Real estate and residential development

- **Job:** understand the building, the location and the homes; register interest or book a viewing.
- **World:** the site plan, floor plates, views from each floor, materials.
- **Structure:** building hero → location → residences (plans, views) → amenities → availability → register.
- **Type:** clean grotesque (Switzer, Instrument Sans) with a refined serif for headings (Sentient, Gambetta).
- **Palette:** Travertine, Sand Dune or Glacier.
- **Imagery:** renders labelled as renders, real location photography.
- **Signature:** a floor picker that shows the view from each level; an interactive site plan; sun path across the day.
- **Motion:** measured and clear; data changes animate without delaying information.
- **Components:** availability table with filters, floor plan viewer, registration form, brochure download.
- **Pitfalls:** renders presented as photos, inaccessible plans, forms that ask too much.

## 7. Automotive, aviation and yachts

- **Job:** feel performance and engineering, configure, book a test or a consultation.
- **World:** the wind tunnel, the cockpit, the hull line, telemetry.
- **Structure:** machine hero → design → performance → interior → configurator → dealers or contact.
- **Type:** wide or condensed grotesque (Archivo width axis, Big Shoulders Display, Anybody; ABC Monument Grotesk or GT America when licensed) with technical numerals.
- **Palette:** Midnight Chrome with a signal accent, or colors from the paint program.
- **Imagery:** studio renders and real motion photography; consistent reflections.
- **Signature:** a procedural airflow field reacting to the pointer; a scrubbed rotation or image sequence; a configurator with real paint colors.
- **Motion:** fast in, precise settle, confident camera moves; technical HUD chrome allowed where the subject is technical.
- **Components:** configurator with price summary, specification comparison, test booking, dealer locator.
- **Pitfalls:** unskippable intro films, specification numbers without units or sources, heavy 3D on mobile.

## 8. Wine, spirits and gastronomy

- **Job:** feel origin and craft, choose a bottle or experience, buy or visit.
- **World:** soil strata, the cellar, the harvest calendar, the label.
- **Structure:** origin hero → the house → range → vintages or recipes → visit → shop (with age gate where required).
- **Type:** characterful serif (Libre Caslon Display, EB Garamond, Boska) with a sturdy sans (Hanken Grotesk).
- **Palette:** Oxblood & Bone, Moss & Brass or Olive Grove.
- **Imagery:** landscape, hands at work, bottles on consistent backgrounds.
- **Signature:** descend through soil strata to each vintage; a harvest calendar as navigation; label details under a loupe.
- **Motion:** slow and grounded; settling layers; no bounce.
- **Components:** age gate, tasting notes, vintage table, club subscription, visit booking.
- **Pitfalls:** dark pages with low-contrast text, age gates that block crawlers entirely, stock vineyard photos.

## 9. Creative studio or agency

- **Job:** prospective clients judge taste and results; talent judges culture; both want the work fast.
- **World:** the studio's own process artifact: the sketchbook, the reel, the grid, the edit.
- **Structure:** work first (index or reel) → case studies → capabilities → studio → contact.
- **Type:** a face that is the identity (Bricolage Grotesque, Unbounded, Anybody; PP Neue Montreal or ABC Favorit when licensed) with a quiet text face.
- **Palette:** the studio's own; often monochrome plus one color, or per-project color.
- **Imagery:** the work itself, consistently framed.
- **Signature:** an index that previews projects on hover and focus; DOM-synced WebGL image effects; case study transitions with shared elements.
- **Motion:** expressive but fast; the work stays on top.
- **Components:** filterable work index, case study template, reel player with posters, careers, contact.
- **Pitfalls:** a long intro before the work, a blob cursor by reflex, unreadable experimental type.

## 10. Personal portfolio

- **Job:** a recruiter or client understands who, what and how good within seconds, then contacts.
- **World:** the person's medium: code, camera, pen, material.
- **Structure:** name, role and best work in the first view → selected work → about → contact.
- **Type:** one strong family, used with range (Bricolage Grotesque, Fraunces, Geist when fitting).
- **Palette:** personal and restrained; Ink & Paper Mono is a safe base.
- **Signature:** one interaction that demonstrates the person's skill in their own medium.
- **Motion:** quick and personal; nothing blocks reading the work.
- **Components:** project list, case study, résumé download, contact links.
- **Pitfalls:** skill bars, generic testimonials, the same template as every other portfolio.

## 11. Premium technology launch

- **Job:** understand what it does, see it working, believe the claim, try or buy.
- **World:** the product's own interface or mechanism.
- **Structure:** product in action → the key capability demonstrated → proof → details → pricing or availability.
- **Type:** precise sans with personality (Geist, Onest, Instrument Sans; Söhne or GT America when licensed) with mono only for code.
- **Palette:** Glacier or the product's own UI palette; avoid purple gradients unless the brand owns them.
- **Imagery:** real screens rebuilt in code, product photography or procedural visuals.
- **Signature:** a live, interactive demonstration; a scroll-driven product sequence; UI rebuilt from tokens that animates element by element.
- **Motion:** snappy UI springs, shared-element transitions, deterministic demos.
- **Components:** feature demos, comparison table, pricing, changelog, sign-up.
- **Pitfalls:** screenshots in browser frames, invented metrics, logo walls of unknown brands.

## 12. Private banking, wealth and fintech

- **Job:** trust, clarity about services and fees, a confident first contact.
- **World:** the ledger, time horizons, the vault, the signature.
- **Structure:** proposition → services → approach → people → insights → contact.
- **Type:** serious serif (Source Serif 4, Tiempos or Publico when licensed) with a precise sans (Schibsted Grotesk, Switzer); tabular numerals everywhere.
- **Palette:** Emerald Private Bank or Ink & Paper Mono.
- **Imagery:** people and places, not stock handshakes.
- **Signature:** a time-horizon slider with clearly labelled illustrative math and assumptions; ledger rails; quiet iris reveals.
- **Motion:** minimal, stable, never playful; data changes without bounce.
- **Components:** calculators with disclosures, advisor profiles, insights index, secure contact.
- **Pitfalls:** implied returns, unlabelled examples, low-contrast legal text.

## 13. Culture: gallery, museum, exhibition, festival

- **Job:** know what is on, when and where; feel the program; book or plan a visit.
- **World:** the exhibition space, the catalog, the poster, the ticket.
- **Structure:** current program → visit information → exhibitions or lineup → collection or archive → tickets.
- **Type:** expressive display for the program (Anybody, Unbounded, Karrik, Basteleur) with a legible text face; festival identities may go maximal.
- **Palette:** Ultramarine Gallery, Cobalt & Porcelain, or per-exhibition color blocks; Neon Night for nightlife and festivals.
- **Imagery:** artworks with rights and captions; installation photography.
- **Signature:** a program poster that recomposes as the user scrolls; a floor map of the exhibition; a lineup grid that reacts to the pointer.
- **Motion:** can be bold; information stays static and readable.
- **Components:** calendar, ticketing, accessibility information, collection search, map.
- **Pitfalls:** opening hours hidden, artwork captions missing, poster-only pages without text.

## 14. Editorial and long-form storytelling

- **Job:** read comfortably, follow an argument, share it.
- **World:** the publication's own format: the issue, the essay, the investigation.
- **Structure:** headline and lede → story with inline media and data → related stories.
- **Type:** a reading serif with optical sizes (Newsreader, Literata, Source Serif 4) and a headline voice; measure 60–70 characters.
- **Palette:** a reading ground with two inks and an accent reserved for links and data.
- **Imagery:** captioned photography, charts rebuilt in code.
- **Signature:** a scrollytelling chart whose states follow the text; a pinned map that updates by paragraph.
- **Motion:** only where it explains; reading sections never animate.
- **Components:** table of contents, footnotes, pull quotes, data graphics with tables, share and cite.
- **Pitfalls:** animation on body text, sticky ads over text, charts without accessible data.
