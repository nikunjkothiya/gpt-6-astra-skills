# Quality Evidence and Acceptance

Use this reference for a substantial final review, a disputed aesthetic decision, or a comparison between implementations. Keep evidence proportional to the task; a single corrected alignment may need one matched view and a brief note.

## Establish the review contract

Write the primary task, distinctive design idea, required states and viewing conditions, and material invariants before judging the result. Distinguish hard requirements from preferences. For an original design, multiple aesthetic solutions can pass; for supplied-reference reconstruction, the supplied relationships constrain the result.

Use the dimensions in the parent skill only where applicable. Add specific anchors: “the product and its price remain visible together while choosing a finish” is stronger than “good hierarchy.” Record observations before explaining their suspected causes.

## Rate what was observed

| Rating | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blocked | The primary task, required truth, or essential access fails. | A finish selector announces a value that the object does not show. |
| Repair | The task works but a material craft or usability requirement fails. | The primary heading wraps into the subject at the required narrow view. |
| Meets | The stated relationship works in the required inspected conditions. | Subject, selected finish, price, and action remain legible across required views. |
| Strong | The result meets requirements and adds a coherent, observable benefit. | Selection lighting reveals the finish differences while preserving stable framing and direct control. |
| Unobserved | Evidence is missing or the required condition was not inspected. | Only source and still frames exist for an interruptible animation. |
| Not applicable | This capability serves no requirement in this artifact. | Assembly geometry on a text-led journal page. |

“Strong” is not necessary in every dimension. A prominent, well-developed idea supported by quiet, precise ordinary states can be more successful than many competing effects. Do not use effect count, preferred color, maximal complexity, or one fashionable composition as proxies for quality.

Keep hard failures visible even if numeric summaries are requested. Exclude unobserved and inapplicable dimensions from averages and report their counts. Averages cannot turn a blocked task into acceptance. Cross-model portability requires actual runs on those models; well-written instructions alone do not establish equivalent results.

## Turn judgment into an actionable finding

Use this compact record when a finding needs to be retained:

```text
Artifact/revision:
View/state/conditions:
Observed relationship:
Task impact and rating:
Likely cause (inference):
Proposed causal correction:
Evidence after correction:
Remaining unknowns:
```

For example: at a 390 CSS-pixel viewport with the menu closed, the third line of the title overlaps the object. This is a composition repair because the title and subject compete for the same area. The suspected cause is a fixed-height hero. Test intrinsic layout or explicit recomposition, then inspect the same view plus enlarged text. Do not prescribe a smaller font before investigating the layout relationship.

## Review an original design fairly

First assess whether the design supports its actual content and user journey. Then assess whether its visual idea can be perceived without explanation, survives beyond the opening view, and connects to the product. Check ordinary states with the same care as the hero: long labels, selected items, focus, empty or unavailable content, loading, and recovery where present.

An unusual treatment earns its prominence when it improves identity, explanation, exploration, or pleasure while keeping the task accessible. Recognizable authorship may come from typography, imagery, color, motion, spatial structure, or their relationships. Review the chosen relationship rather than demanding all of them at once.

## Decide when to stop

Accept the requested scope when hard requirements pass, material findings are resolved, adjacent affected states have been checked, and the available observations support the claimed quality. Preserve optional taste differences as options rather than extending work indefinitely.

When time or tools prevent a required observation, mark visual or behavioral acceptance as pending for that condition. Deliver completed work with the exact missing check and its consequence. Do not relabel unobserved work as verified or invent awards, user studies, accessibility certification, performance results, or model equivalence.

Stop broad retesting after the relevant checks pass. Reopen a passed condition when a later change affects it, a concrete regression appears, or a required gate remains outstanding.
